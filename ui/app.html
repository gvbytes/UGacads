      A roadmap you can check

      APTG builds a semester-by-semester plan from IIT Kanpur's published templates, schedules and UG Manual. Every placement carries the reason it is there, and every rejection names the constraint responsible.

      1. Set your batch, programme and current semester.
      2. Tick the courses you have already cleared.
      3. Add a target minor, a workload ceiling or a career interest.
      • Prerequisites Evaluated as a boolean expression over everything completed or scheduled earlier.
      • Curriculum sequencing No course is placed before the semester its own template assigns it.
      • Semester availability A course seen only in odd terms cannot sit in an even semester.
      • Credit limits Your ceiling, capped by the UG Manual's 65 — and 27 in the summer term.
      • Eligibility Batch windows, departmental CPI cutoffs, and the rule that a minor may not be in your own department.
      • Maximum duration Capped per programme and batch by UG Manual 3.2.
