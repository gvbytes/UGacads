Remarks

  • The BTM-IS template replaces 27 DE + 27 OE credits with 54 MTB (management track basket) credits
  • A list of courses that will be notified as being eligible for MTB credits will be communicated at the time of application for the BTM program.
  • BTM-IS students can take up to 4 UGPs, but only at most 3 UGPs (27 credits) can be counted towards graduation requirements. All UGPs are optional.
  • Up to 36 OE credits may be waived from the minimum requirements for students opting for either the Dual Degree or Double Major programs.
